Croquis (*Crow-KEY*) an open-source transit sketch planning and GTFS production tool. It was designed to rapidly produce realistic models of proposed or real networks and schedules. This R package and Shiny App includes the following functionalities:
 
* Upload any GTFS or rapidly create one from scratch. 
* Create routes on an interactive map. Draw paths along the road network or freehand between stops.
* Batch-apply service windows, speeds, and headways across your routes to build a complete and valid GTFS in record-time.
* Toggle speeds and headways by hour and speeds across route segments to reflect real-world variability.
* Generate operating cost estimates.

Croquis is available in English, French, and Spanish.
