The future of transit planning is open and democratic.

Comotive harnesses open software to design transit systems that better meet the needs of the communities they serve.

We are eager to serve agencies, consultancies, researchers, and any organisation that plans public transportation with our expertise in:
* Transit network redesign
* Custom transportation planning software and dashboards
* Accessibility to jobs and other analysis using GIS and census data
* Survey design and analysis

**About Julian Villafuerte**

Julian is a Canadian transit planner based in Montréal.

During his Masters at McGill, he was introduced to the universe of open-source transportation planning software in R, including packages like [r5r](https://ipeagit.github.io/r5r/) which enable routing on real and planned networks entirely using open data. 

Julian joined the Société de Transport de Montréal (STM) as a Transit Planner in 2023. He collaborated with other planners on the [2026 bus network redesign](https://www.stm.info/fr/a-propos/grands-projets/grands-projets-bus/refonte-du-reseau-bus). The methods he developed for bus stop spacing optimization were foundational to interventions on lines 18, 24, 51, 161, and 189 which now offer shorter and more reliable trips to customers as a result. 

Julian felt the gap for accessible tools that compare the costs and benefits of transit network design scenarios. To address this, he developed and released [Croquis](https://croquis.comotive.net/), an open-source transit sketch planning and GTFS production software.

Julian is the co-founder of [Activate Transit Windsor-Essex](https://activatetransitwindsoressex.ca/), a transit advocacy group in his hometown. In 2022, he led the development of a [community mandate for transit](https://activatetransitwindsoressex.ca/community-mandate-2022/) based on the results of a survey he designed.

Julian is fluent in English, French and Spanish.
